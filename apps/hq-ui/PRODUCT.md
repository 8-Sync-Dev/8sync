# Product

## Register

product

## Users
The owner of an 8 Sync machine (and later their team) running several Claude Code sessions at once through `8sync ccc`, often while coding in other windows. They glance at HQ to know which session is working, which one waits for them, which is stuck, and to send a message or a task, without reading transcripts.

## Product Purpose
A local-only team view for official Claude Code sessions: who is doing what right now, what needs the owner, and one-click access to chat, terminal and tasks. Success: the state of every session is understood in about two seconds, and the owner never misses a session waiting on them.

## Brand Personality
Clear, alive, calm. A small pixel office where each session is a coworker at a desk: friendly and a little playful, but the information comes first. Voice: short, concrete, Vietnamese-friendly, no buzzwords.

## Anti-references
Generic admin dashboards with stat cards and tables of uuids; walls of log text; cream/beige SaaS; neon cyberpunk overload; game UIs where the fun hides the status.

## Design Principles
1. State at a glance: animation + label + one line, never color alone.
2. The owner's attention is the scarce resource: "waiting for you" and "stuck" always surface first.
3. Show, don't log: details on demand, not on the first screen.
4. Light on the machine: animation pauses when hidden, nothing polls harder than needed.
5. Practice what we preach: local-first, no model calls from the UI.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA contrast (≥ 4.5:1 body). Every state has a text label. `prefers-reduced-motion` shows static frames. Keyboard reachable desks and panels. Vietnamese text renders with Be Vietnam Pro.
